# Preliminary Business Plan — Concept Only

## Executive concept
Explore the development potential of founder-owned land for a mixed-use landmark project, with a 40-story concept and a 60-floor aspirational alternative, including underground facilities only if legal and technically feasible. The business is not yet validated.

## Potential program (illustrative, not decided)
- Office / flexible workspace
- Residential or hospitality, if allowed and supported by market evidence
- Retail, food, and services at ground level
- Shared meeting, event, or amenity spaces
- Parking, bicycle storage, loading, plant, and utility infrastructure
- Basement/underground functions only after geotechnical, groundwater, access, code, cost, and environmental review

The eventual mix must be based on local demand, zoning, affordability, market studies, and design.

## Founder value proposition
The land may potentially be a contribution to a development partnership, subject to verified title, valuation, encumbrances, and market demand. Land ownership alone does not establish sufficient project equity, financing, or entitlement.

## Development strategy
1. Low-cost validation and documentation.
2. Professional planning and site feasibility.
3. Partner and capital exploration.
4. Entitlement, design, and financing.
5. Construction and operation, if viable.

## Capital strategy (potential routes)
- Land contribution to a joint venture (subject to valuation and legal structuring).
- Experienced developer/operator partner.
- Equity investors, with compliant disclosure and legal review.
- Construction lending after lenders' prerequisites are met.
- Phased or smaller project to reduce capital requirements.
- Eligible public incentives only through their legitimate application procedures.
- Pre-leasing or pre-sales only when legally permitted, properly disclosed, and commercially credible.

## What is not assumed
No investor, lender, tenant, AI firm, contractor, or public agency has expressed interest or made a commitment. No budget or return forecast is validated. No funding is guaranteed.

## Investor readiness
Before an investor pitch, assemble:
- Parcel/title and ownership evidence (redact sensitive information)
- Survey and planning/zoning memo
- Feasibility and market studies
- Preliminary concept, scope and schedule
- Independent cost plan and contingency
- Capital stack, assumptions, and sensitivity model
- Risk register and mitigation
- Entity and governance plan
- Clear disclosure of unresolved approvals and risks

## Alternatives that must be compared
- 40-floor concept
- 60-floor concept
- Smaller tower / mid-rise
- Low-rise or mixed-use redevelopment
- Phased development
- Joint venture with established developer
- Sale or ground lease of land, if appropriate
- No-build / hold option

## Ethical and legal commitments
No bypassing government rules, falsifying financial or property records, misrepresenting approvals, or promising investor returns. Do not imply AI firms will pay rent or fund construction unless a written agreement exists.
