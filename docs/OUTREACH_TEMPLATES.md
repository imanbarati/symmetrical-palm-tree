# Outreach Templates — Preliminary and Non-Binding

These are introductory discussion templates, not securities offers, financing solicitations, legal offers, or commitments. Have local counsel review them before external use. Replace brackets with verified facts only. Do not send unverified claims.

## 1. Development partner introduction

**Subject:** Preliminary discussion — potential land-backed development opportunity in [city]

Hello [Name],

I am the owner / reported owner of a land parcel in [city / district] and am exploring its lawful development potential. I am at an early feasibility stage and have not yet established that a high-rise concept is permitted, financeable, or technically viable.

My initial scenarios are a 40-story mixed-use concept, a more ambitious 60-floor alternative if planning and technical evidence support it, and smaller or phased options. Underground levels are optional and subject to geotechnical, groundwater, access, regulatory and cost review.

I am seeking an experienced development partner to advise whether a land-contribution joint venture, long-term ground lease, or another structure could be appropriate. I would welcome an initial capability discussion covering relevant completed projects, local planning experience, preferred deal structures, and the feasibility work you would require.

I can provide a redacted site summary after basic mutual fit is established. No exclusivity, transfer, financing, or project commitment is being offered at this stage.

Regards,
[Founder name]
[Contact details]

## 2. Professional feasibility inquiry

**Subject:** Request for staged proposal — land and high-rise feasibility review

Hello [Firm / Professional],

I am evaluating the development potential of a land parcel in [city]. I need a staged, costed proposal for an initial review covering [survey / planning / geotechnical desktop review / market / quantity surveying].

Please specify:
- Scope, methodology, deliverables and exclusions.
- Licenses, relevant project experience and named responsible professionals.
- Inputs needed from me and site access requirements.
- Fee breakdown, taxes, disbursements and payment milestones.
- Expected dependencies and realistic delivery schedule.
- Professional indemnity insurance and limitations.
- Conflicts of interest and references.

Please distinguish a desktop opinion from formal reports, authority advice, valuation, or signed engineering work.

Regards,
[Founder name]

## 3. Potential technology tenant / AI company

**Subject:** Future workspace requirements — non-binding market sounding

Hello [Company / Real Estate Team],

I am studying a possible mixed-use development in [city]. The project is at an early feasibility stage, and no building approval, construction funding, delivery date, or available tenancy is confirmed.

I am researching whether technology companies may have future requirements for office, research, collaboration, data infrastructure, or other compliant workspace in this market. Would your team be willing to share general location, size, infrastructure, sustainability and lease requirements for future consideration?

This is a market-research inquiry only. It is not an offer of space, request for financing, or proposal for barter / in-kind payment. Any future lease or service arrangement would be separately negotiated, valued, and documented.

Regards,
[Founder name]

## 4. Investor / capital partner introduction

**Subject:** Preliminary capital-partner discussion — feasibility stage land opportunity

Hello [Name],

I am investigating the potential development of a land parcel in [city]. The project is currently at the information and feasibility stage. Land title, planning parameters, market demand, cost, valuation and development scale require independent verification.

I am exploring whether a qualified capital partner or development investor would consider funding a defined feasibility stage or evaluating a future joint venture after diligence. I am not representing that the project is approved, funded, or investment-ready, and I am not making an offer of securities or promising returns.

Could you share your investment criteria, geographic focus, minimum diligence requirements, preferred stage of entry, and whether you consider land-contribution structures?

Regards,
[Founder name]

## Before sending any template
- Confirm jurisdiction and whether local law regulates introductions, investment promotions, broker activity or securities solicitation.
- Verify every statement and insert only substantiated information.
- Do not attach private title or identity records to unsolicited messages.
- Do not describe informal feedback as investor interest or a commitment.
- Record who was contacted, date, recipient role, response, permission to follow up, and next action.
- Obtain legal review before discussing investment terms, accepting funds, exclusivity or land rights.
