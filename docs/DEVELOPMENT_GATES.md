# Development Decision Gates

Use this as a stop/go control document. Passing one gate does not imply that later approvals or financing will follow.

| Gate | Required evidence | Decision-maker / reviewer | If gate fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Brief | Founder objectives, city/country, approximate parcel area, preferred alternatives | Founder / coordinator | Clarify scope; no external claims |
| 1 — Title | Official title review, boundary and rights checks, liens and transferability | Local real-estate counsel / surveyor | Resolve, restructure, or stop |
| 2 — Planning | Current zoning, height/density, permitted uses, written authority guidance, entitlement path | Planning consultant / counsel | Reduce scale, pursue lawful process, or stop |
| 3 — Site | Survey, geotechnical/environmental screening, groundwater, utilities and access | Licensed professionals | Redesign, omit basement, or stop |
| 4 — Market | Comparable evidence, demand, achievable rents/prices and target tenant analysis | Independent market advisor | Change use/scale, phase, or stop |
| 5 — Economics | Independent cost plan, model, contingency, schedule, residual land value and downside case | Quantity surveyor / financial model reviewer | Re-price, reduce scope, seek partner, or stop |
| 6 — Partner and capital | Verified partner track record, funding evidence, terms, capital gap and governance | Counsel / financial advisor / founder | Do not grant exclusivity or encumber land |
| 7 — Approvals and delivery | All required approvals, signed contracts, financing, insurance, construction safety and professional appointments | Authorities / lender / project governance | Do not commence construction |
| 8 — Operations | Commissioning, occupancy permissions, operating team, tenant contracts and reserve funding | Owner / operator / authorities | Do not open or occupy |

## Non-negotiable controls
- No construction or occupancy without required approvals and safety sign-offs.
- No misrepresentation to investors, lenders, tenants, authorities or counterparties.
- No transfer, mortgage, option, exclusivity or guarantee without independent legal advice.
- No reliance on unawarded grants or uncommitted loans.
- No use of AI-company names or logos to imply endorsement, tenancy or financing.
- No use of a conceptual floor count as evidence of legal buildability.
