# HANDOFF — Land-to-Tower Development Initiative

## Goal
Help a land-owning founder with limited cash investigate the lawful, realistic development potential of their parcel, with an aspirational 40-story concept and a 60-floor alternate scenario, underground levels subject to feasibility.

## Status at handoff
- Repository initialized with planning documents.
- Founder says they own land and have limited funds.
- Location, parcel size, title documentation, zoning, valuation, land condition, and financial details are not yet verified.
- No company formation, approvals, capital, partners, leases, or repayment arrangements are confirmed.
- No feasibility claim should be made yet.

## Immediate inputs to request
Only request what is necessary and avoid publishing sensitive details:
1. City/country and approximate parcel area.
2. Public-facing parcel identifier or redacted survey, if safe to share.
3. Evidence of ownership/title and liens—review privately with professionals, not in this public repository.
4. Existing zoning and any planning designation.
5. Site shape, access, road frontage, existing structures, utility access, and any known constraints.
6. Founder’s project objectives and acceptable smaller/phased alternatives.

## First 30-day work plan (adjust to local conditions)
### Phase 0: Information and feasibility triage
- Collect parcel facts, verify title privately, survey and planning records.
- Identify the correct planning authority and legal jurisdiction.
- Determine permitted use, height, floor-area ratio/density, setbacks, parking, access, and entitlement process.
- Build a list of missing information and preliminary risks.

### Phase 1: Low-cost pre-development
- Seek preliminary planning consultation where available.
- Obtain quotes for survey, planning, geotechnical/environmental screening, and feasibility work.
- Ask experienced local developers whether a land-contribution JV is plausible.
- Compare tower ambition against smaller/low-rise/phased/JV alternatives.
- Prepare preliminary cost and market evidence.

### Phase 2: Decision and capital strategy
- Independent feasibility review.
- Identify capital sources, their requirements, and project equity gap.
- Prepare truthful investor/JV presentation and evidence room.
- No solicitations or financial promises without legal review.

### Phase 3: Formal development
Proceed only after appropriate title, planning, design, engineering, contracts, capital, permits, and construction safeguards are in place.

## Potential corporate path (not jurisdiction-specific advice)
- A founder-owned holding entity or project SPV may be considered after local counsel and tax review.
- Evaluate whether the land can be contributed or leased into a JV; obtain valuation and legal advice.
- Define ownership, governance, decision rights, capital calls, dilution, liabilities, exit, disputes, and lender protections in written agreements.
- Avoid transferring land or signing commitments until advised independently.
