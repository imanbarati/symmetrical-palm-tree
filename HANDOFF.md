# HANDOFF — Land-to-Tower Development Initiative

## Goal
Help a land-owning founder with limited cash investigate the lawful, realistic development potential of their parcel, with an aspirational 40-story concept and a 60-floor alternate scenario, underground levels subject to feasibility.

## Status at handoff
- Repository contains initial project, AI-agent, business-plan and evaluation documents plus a feasibility/funding/financial diligence package.
- Founder reports owning land and having limited funds.
- Location, parcel size, title documentation, zoning, valuation, land condition, market, currency, and financial details are not yet verified.
- No company formation, approvals, capital, partners, leases, or repayment arrangements are confirmed.
- No feasibility claim should be made yet.
- The financial model currently consists of formulas/specification and blank CSV inputs; no project returns or construction cost have been calculated.

## Immediate founder inputs (do not request public disclosure of sensitive documents)
1. City and country.
2. Approximate parcel area and dimensions.
3. Whether the founder has an official title document; share sensitive details only privately with a lawyer, not in this public repository.
4. Existing zoning / land-use information, if known.
5. Road access, utility access, site shape, existing structures and known constraints.
6. Whether the founder would consider a smaller project, ground lease, JV, or sale if a tower is not viable.

## Next work sequence
### Priority 1 — Site and title
- Obtain city/country and approximate parcel size from founder.
- Identify the correct planning authority from official sources.
- Privately verify title, encumbrances, easements, parcel boundaries and legal ability to contribute, lease, sell or finance the land.
- Do not upload unredacted title, identity or financial records to this repository.

### Priority 2 — Planning and technical
- Obtain official zoning, land-use, permitted-use, floor-area, height, setback, coverage, parking, access and public-process requirements.
- Ask whether the 40-story scenario has any lawful entitlement pathway; treat 60 floors as a separate unproven option.
- Commission staged survey, geotechnical, groundwater, utilities, environmental and basement feasibility work only when justified.

### Priority 3 — Market and model
- Research current local comparables and demand.
- Obtain local professional cost estimates and a project schedule.
- Populate data/project_inputs_template.csv with dated, sourced values.
- Build and independently review a formula-driven model using the specification in docs/FINANCIAL_MODEL_SPEC.md.
- Compare tower, reduced-scale, phased, lease/JV, sale and no-build scenarios.

### Priority 4 — Partner and funding
- Prepare a redacted, fact-checked site teaser.
- Shortlist development partners based on completed comparable projects, financial capability, references and integrity.
- Explore land-contribution JV, ground lease, sale, equity, and debt in the correct sequence.
- Do not assume a land contribution equals cash equity or that any lender accepts it as collateral.
- Do not assume any AI/technology company will fund construction or become a tenant.

### Priority 5 — Formal development
Proceed only after appropriate title, planning, design, engineering, contracts, capital, permits, insurance and construction safeguards are in place.

## Potential corporate path (not jurisdiction-specific advice)
A founder-owned holding entity or project SPV may be considered after local counsel and tax review. Evaluate land contribution, lease, option or JV structures with independent valuation. Define ownership, governance, decision rights, capital calls, dilution, liabilities, security, exit and disputes in written agreements. Avoid transferring land, signing an option, granting exclusivity, mortgaging property or giving guarantees until independently advised.

## AI/technology providers
Potential future tenants or vendors are unconfirmed. No company is committed. Any rent, service-credit, equity or in-kind compensation requires a viable permitted project, independent valuation, negotiation, accounting/tax review, legal review and signed agreement. Do not include speculative AI tenancy as committed revenue or funding.

Each agent must update this document with date, work completed, sources, assumptions, risks, decisions and next actions. Keep public material redacted. Never silently overwrite contradictory evidence. The project remains HOLD until required evidence supports a different decision.
