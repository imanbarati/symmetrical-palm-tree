# Land-to-Tower Development Initiative

A legally compliant feasibility and development framework for exploring a high-rise mixed-use project on land owned by the project founder.

## Vision
Explore the feasibility of a landmark tower concept: **40 stories / up to 60 floors as an alternate scenario**, plus underground levels. These are aspirational scenarios—not approved height, design, capacity, or construction commitments. The project's first goal is to determine what is legally, technically, commercially, and financially possible on the actual parcel.

## Founder starting position
- Founder reports owning land and having limited personal capital.
- No funding, planning approval, construction, tenants, or AI-service repayment is assumed secured.
- Land ownership must be independently verified and its title, liens, boundaries, and valuation reviewed.

## Core strategy
1. Verify title and parcel details; commission a survey.
2. Determine jurisdiction, zoning, height limits, allowable floor area, setbacks, access, parking, and planning process.
3. Evaluate compliant alternatives: rezoning, variance, planning amendment, public consultation, or a smaller project.
4. Commission staged feasibility work before spending heavily.
5. Build a credible financing package and approach experienced developers, lenders, investors, and potential tenants.
6. Use a transparent corporation/SPV and legally reviewed contracts.
7. Keep AI/technology partners' proposed in-kind compensation strictly hypothetical until negotiated, valued, documented, and approved.

## Scenarios
- **Scenario A:** 40-story tower, subject to feasibility and lawful approvals.
- **Scenario B:** 60-floor aspirational option, only if lawful and economically/technically feasible.
- **Scenario C:** Smaller, phased, mixed-use, low-rise, or joint-venture alternative.
- **Underground:** basement levels are not presumed feasible; geotechnical, groundwater, utilities, access, code, and cost must be assessed.

## Decision gates
No irreversible expenditure or construction before the relevant approvals, contracts, financing, and safety reviews. See [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md), [EVALUATION.md](EVALUATION.md), and [BUSINESS_PLAN.md](BUSINESS_PLAN.md).

## Disclaimer
This repository is planning and research material, not legal, architectural, engineering, investment, tax, or lending advice. Never fabricate approvals, investor interest, valuations, cash flows, collateral, tenant commitments, or project credentials. Do not bypass rules or safety requirements.
